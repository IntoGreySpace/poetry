Date = "2026-03-15"

Surfing downstream\
invites the exercise\
of every core lesson\
you need to know.

Balance in the moment\
flowing with the current\
opportunity to progress\
leaving others behind

nimble in the waves\
flexing with the bumps\
the friction as you drag\
in those bottom-out shoals.

Balancing those moments\
with the need\
to look downstream\
planning the course\
to find your flow\
and steer clear\
of dangers ever near.

Always stealing glances\
at nature's gleaming branches\
so many paths not taken\
so many futures foresaken

you only have one ride\
so you need to focus\
the current always guiding\
your downstream glide.
