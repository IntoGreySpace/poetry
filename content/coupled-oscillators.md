The waves of grief\
and relief\
rolling in tides\
powerfully surging.

Driven by a couple\
thrashing in tragedy\
flying apart\
and back together

seeking justification\
for losing something
that felt at least okay\
and sometimes great.

Was it really so terrible\
when the present moments\
are palpably so?\
Couldn't it have been\
at least bearable?

But when the anger\
inevitably comes\
the unseen current\
of suspicious liquids\
is terribly real.

And reel you do\
veering off course\
roiling turmoil\
in a once-placid sea.

You need to finally see\
the whole chart\
to begin a new start\
on solid ground\
unbound\
finally\
from that churning sea.
