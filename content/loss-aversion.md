Name = "Loss Aversion"
Date = "2026-01-24"
+++

Loss,\
a version of myself\
shuffles away

We don't like to lose\
even things we got\
only yesterday

Once it mingles\
with your neural signals\
that new stranger\
becomes a part

So when you fly to pieces\
it hurts\
like blunt force trauma\
like losing your mama.

Can't you just unwind?\
go backwards in your mind\
to the time before\
it entered your door?

Apparently not,\
because I've tried\
and that version remains\
coursing through my veins.
