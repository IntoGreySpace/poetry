Date = "2026-09-06"

Is love all you really need?\
Is it really easy?

All these love songs\
made me cringe\
until I found\
myself\
in love.

Maybe love\
is what we all need\
but sadly\
it isn't easy.

No amount of effort\
can will it.\
It must be found\
not forced.

It emerges\
organically\
as an attractor\
chaotic\
and alive.

Parents should always give it\
but they too\
are human.

And thus it starts\
at the beginning\
that unmet yearning\
for something\
unconditional.

Casting a dark shadow\
a prophecy\
an insatiable\
black hole.

If you can't accept\
yourself\
why should I?

"All the lonely people"\
is the tragic refrain\
that reigns\
in the end.
