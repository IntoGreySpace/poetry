I used to be centered\
a perfect circle\
orbiting one point\
always stable\
but so sterile.

Now that I've met you,\
what do I do?

Stretch that center\
to accommodate,\
like an awkward smile?

Or do I become\
an ellipse\
with two centers\
one for me\
and one for we?

That second center tracking\
our strange attractor\
chaotic, dynamic, alive.

The first center\
anchors the other\
keeping our shape\
as we undulate.

Am I brave enough\
to hold them both\
here in my heart?

If I don't\
then surely\
we'll fly apart.

I simply need to trust\
that we won't collapse\
to that flat line\
where all shape is lost.

Finally feeling\
your body\
next to mine\
will be a start!
