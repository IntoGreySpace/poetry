Date = "2026-03-13"

Steps beget the fall\
yet it seems so easy\
to focus\
one upon the next\
just don't look at them all!

Like Orpheus\
our faith is lacking\
in the path ahead\
begging the fall.

Always tempting fate\
scanning ahead\
focusing on the landing\
and missing that step\
that sends you flailing.

And yet, foresight is key\
to plan the path\
and adapt to traps\
the blind prophet is a myth\
Tiresias, Orpheus and the lot.

There is no secret\
in the end\
you just have to try\
and center your self\
on each step\
no matter how wobbly\
you feel\
your way\
ever onward\
falls and all.
