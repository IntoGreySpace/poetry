Name = "Strange Manifold"
Date = "2026-04-03"
+++

Staring into the blue daydream sky\
my mind aloft\
thinking of the future\
my current stature\
the level abstract

Suddenly, I slip below\
and see that sky rip open\
peering between molecules\
the secrets unseen

How can I listlessly dream\
when this dark world\
lurks beneath\
what seems?

Delusions abound\
while reality is all around\
invisible, flowing waves\
appearing as light

as those thoughts aflight

bouncing like swells\
between gravitational wells

I must bridge these worlds\
forging a pathway between\
manifold facts\
and many strong desires

Integrating shadows,\
light, dreams,

everything it seems

must fit inside\
this many-folded brain\
with pathways branching\
and axons flashing\
ions flowing\
into knowing

the state is unstable

always moving\
attractors beckon\
and so I slide\
ever on

grasping only these traces

of those electric places\
my mind goes\
gliding along\
this strange manifold
