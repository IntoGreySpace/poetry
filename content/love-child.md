Date = "2026-04-14"

Our love was born\
from our spirit seeds\
like a child\
with no body\
she lives within\
the charged ether\
that emergent space\
of our daily interaction.

We each must nurture\
this shared gift\
we give each other\
each the lover\
each the loved.

This child lives\
as long as love flows\
how long that is\
neither of us knows.

So we cherish it now\
feeling it grow\
feeding it presence\
attention, and care.

Like a real child\
she's possessed by none\
her own life she's won\
unpredictable and alive.

We are merely guardians\
bound by fate\
so until your next kiss\
I patiently wait.
