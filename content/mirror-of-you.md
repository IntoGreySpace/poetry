Gazing in the mirror\
of you\
reflects my worries\
of me

This shiny wall\
separates us all\
from each other\
from ourselves

It's barely OK\
by myself\
to be\
myself

But when I'm bare\
naked\
with you\
I'm fully exposed\
stripped\
of full control

How can you love me\
when I don't?

How can I love you\
when I'm lost\
in my own cloud\
of doubt?

How can we break through\
to each other\
to ourselves\
to love, true?

Acceptance\
humility\
sharing\
trust\
understanding

These words sound true\
so easily said\
so hard to do\
but truly\
I feel it with you.
