Date = "2026-04-17"
+++

Somehow, I awoke\
to find myself\
homeless\
adrift\
at sea.

The romantic notion\
of this open ocean\
espoused in books\
is not as fun\
as it looks.

I always imagined\
how tough I'd be\
when something hard\
finally happens\
to me.

But maybe I'm too soft\
too weak\
after a long life\
of just reading\
and writing\
poetry.
