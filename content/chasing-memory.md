Name = "Chasing a Memory"
Date = "2026-07-16"
+++

That magical ski run\
or unique food experience\
shines like a beacon\
pushing you on

You want even more\
and better\
but in fact\
all you get\
is wanting

That memory\
is just a snapshot\
a stale cartoon\
of a vanished\
moment

Chasing it is like\
chasing time itself\
you're always behind\
playing catch-up\
tweaking the wake\
of the passing ship

Climb aboard\
get in front\
look forward\
not back

Leave the past\
to reflection

Open your heart\
to the presents\
showered\
with uncertainty\
upon your path.

It will not be\
the same\
it never is\
it will just be\
whatever is.
