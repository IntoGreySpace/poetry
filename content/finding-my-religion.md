Name = "Finding My Religion"
Date = "2026-06-12"
+++

As an atheist,\
this is unexpected\
but somehow\
I've genuflected

not to a God\
and certainly\
not a Priest!

No, I've found\
something else\
something so true\
it must be so

Something so pure\
like water\
if flows

I can't always find it\
especially if I try\
I need to look askance\
and catch it by chance

This religion of mine\
is all in my head\
and with me\
it'll soon be dead

This religion is indeed\
75% water\
and all the rest

I know it from the calm\
I can sometimes feel\
when I reset\
reflect\
and allow myself

to know\
that everything\
I've suspected\
is actually true

Religion is a feeling\
of truth\
without proof\
so after years of proving\
I'm ready to feel\
that proofless truth

Even though my truth\
grows each day\
some things stay\
and these fixtures\
so easily discounted\
should really be counted

So still\
and transparent\
they are barely seen\
lurking in the dark

which is where\
you'll remain\
if seeking my truths

go find your own\
before its too late!
